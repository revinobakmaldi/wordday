## Design Context

### Users
WordDay is for curious, time-poor Gen Z adults who want to expand their vocabulary in tiny daily moments. They primarily encounter the product on an iPhone Home Screen or Lock Screen, then open the app for a definition, example, quick browse, or one-tap learned state. The experience must deliver value in seconds and never feel like homework.

### Brand Personality
Clever, energizing, approachable. WordDay should make language feel current and culturally alive while retaining the credibility of a well-edited reference book. The voice is concise, lightly witty, and encouraging without streak pressure, gamification guilt, or classroom formality.

### Aesthetic Direction
**After Dark** is the selected visual identity. It combines deep aubergine surfaces, warm cream type, acid-lime orientation cues, condensed display typography, and the confidence of a late-night culture journal. A cropped orbital ring is the signature graphic device, used sparingly on app and widget surfaces.

The interface is intentionally dark rather than a reversible light/dark theme. It prioritizes native iOS performance, readable type, strong contrast, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels, 44-point touch targets, and reduced-motion support. Avoid decorative glow, generic glass cards, blue-purple gradients, corporate productivity styling, childish classroom motifs, and guilt-driven streak mechanics.

### Design Principles
1. **Meaning before chrome:** the word, definition, and example dominate every screen.
2. **Reward ten seconds:** the core learning loop must be understandable and completable at a glance.
3. **Editorial, not academic:** use expressive typography and composition without making vocabulary feel like coursework.
4. **One vivid accent:** each look has a restrained base and one memorable accent used for action and orientation.
5. **Calm progress:** learned state is immediate and satisfying, but never coercive or competitive.
